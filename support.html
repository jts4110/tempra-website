Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Tempra asking for my location?

Tempra uses your location to automatically show weather for where you are on the Home screen. You can deny this and still use the app - just add cities manually from the Cities tab instead, or turn location off entirely in Settings.

How do I add a city to my favorites?

From the Cities tab, tap the search bar and type a city name. Tap a result from the list to save it. Saved cities sync automatically across your devices.

How do I compare two cities side by side?

On the Cities tab, turn on "Compare cities side-by-side," then tap any saved city to open the comparison view.

How do badges/achievements work?

Badges unlock automatically as you use the app - for example, checking the weather during a thunderstorm, or saving cities from three different continents. You can see all available badges and which ones you've earned on the Badges tab.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and everything in it, and cannot be undone. See our account deletion page for full details.
