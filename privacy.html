Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026

Tempra is developed by Jonathan Smith ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information Tempra collects, how it's used, and how you can control or delete it.

Information We Collect

Location

If you grant permission, Tempra uses your device's GPS location to automatically show weather for where you are. Your location is sent to our weather data provider (Open-Meteo) solely to retrieve a forecast for those coordinates, and is not stored on our servers or linked to your account. You can deny location access and still use Tempra by adding cities manually.

Account Information

Tempra uses Supabase (a backend service provider) to manage accounts. Depending on how you sign in, this may include an email address, or, if you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, the private relay email Apple provides instead. We do not receive your real email address in that case.

App Data

To provide the app's features, we store: cities you've saved as favorites, your achievement/badge progress, and your app settings/preferences (such as units and notification toggles). This data is stored in our Supabase database, tied to your account, and syncs across your devices.

Third-Party Services

Tempra relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • Open-Meteo - weather forecast and geocoding data. Receives coordinates only, not tied to your identity.
  • RainViewer - live precipitation radar imagery shown on the Map tab. No personal data is sent.
  • OpenStreetMap - base map tiles shown on the Map tab. No personal data is sent.
  • Supabase - hosts our backend, database, and authentication system.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings > Delete Account within the app. See our account deletion page for details. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Children's Privacy

Tempra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and revise the effective date above.
