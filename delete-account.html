Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Tempra account and all associated data directly within the app. This cannot be undone.

Steps

  1. Open Tempra and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

What gets deleted: your account, all saved cities, badge/achievement progress, and app settings. This happens immediately and permanently - there is no recovery period.

Trouble deleting your account?

If you're unable to access the app to delete your account yourself, email us at tempraweatherapp@outlook.com from the email address associated with your account and we'll delete it on your behalf.
